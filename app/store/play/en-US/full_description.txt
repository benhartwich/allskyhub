allskyhub is the app for the allskyhub all-sky camera: a Raspberry Pi with an all-sky camera that records the whole sky by day and night. The camera connects to allskyhub.org by itself: no port forwarding, no FTP, nothing to install.

YOUR SKY, ALWAYS WITH YOU
• Live image of the whole sky, day and night
• Timelapse, keogram and star trails of every night
• Gallery with full-size images and the pictures of recent nights

WHAT HAPPENS IN THE SKY
• The camera detects meteors, aurora and lightning by itself
• Satellites and aircraft are recognised and named instead of counted as meteors
• Sky view with constellations, meteors and overflights, also as an AR mode over your phone's camera

WEATHER AND CAMERA
• Clouds now and for the next hour, outlook for tonight
• The camera's readings such as sky brightness and temperature, statistics over the years
• The camera's health at a glance, with hints when something is wrong

EASY TO SET UP
• Pair the camera with a QR code and bring it onto your Wi-Fi
• Change the camera's settings right in the app
• Sharing, a public page and the camera network, if you like

You need an allskyhub camera and an account on allskyhub.org. Request access at allskyhub.org/zugang; the parts list and the guide are at allskyhub.org/hardware and allskyhub.org/allsky.

allskyhub is open source: github.com/benhartwich/allskyhub
